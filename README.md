# Battle of Spades

**Current version:** Alpha 0.2.0.1

**Status:** Alpha. Battle of Spades is still being made: new things arrive often, some things will change, and you may meet a bug or two. Your rank, unlocks and loadouts carry over from one version to the next.

![Battle of Spades: Conquest on Caspian Border](screenshots/battlefield.png)

## What is it?

Battle of Spades is Ace of Spades 0.75 brought back, then grown into a Battlefield-style war.

At its heart is the classic game, rule for rule: a blocky world under pale blue fog, blue against green. Every block can be dug out with your spade or built back up in any colour, so a hill becomes a trench, a tunnel or a fort while the match is on. Fight with the Rifle, the SMG or the Shotgun, steal the enemy's intel, and hold the command posts.

Then the maps get big. Conquest is fought over flags and tickets across 21 Battlefield maps up to 1,024 blocks wide, in homage to Battlefield 2: Modern Combat, Bad Company, Bad Company 2 and Battlefield 3, with tanks, APCs, jeeps, dune buggies, anti-air, helicopters and boats. Pick one of Battlefield 3's four kits, fight in a squad, revive your friends, rank up from Private to Colonel, and unlock 70 weapons, their attachments, gadgets and outfits as you go.

Play on your own against bots that fight like people and dig, build and tunnel like Ace of Spades players do, with up to four people on one screen, or with friends over your home network, online, or on a dedicated server for up to 64 players.

## Features

- **Classic Ace of Spades:** the 0.75/0.76 rules, ported from OpenSpades: digging, building with the colour palette, Rifle, SMG, Shotgun and grenades, Capture the Intel and Territorial Control, classic generated maps and any `.vxl` map.
- **Conquest:** Battlefield battles on big maps, with flags, tickets and Battlefield-sized motor pools: tanks, APCs, jeeps, the BF2: Modern Combat dune buggies, anti-air, attack and transport helicopters, and boats.
- **Team Deathmatch:** infantry only, first to 100 kills, fought in a combat area in the middle of the big maps (or across the whole of a classic map).
- **Battlefield 3 kits and armory:** Assault, Engineer, Support and Recon; 13 assault rifles, 9 carbines, 11 LMGs, 14 sniper and marksman rifles, 9 PDWs, 6 shotguns and 8 sidearms; optics, barrels, underbarrels and ammo; two gadget slots (defibrillator, M320, M26 MASS, RPG, SMAW, AT mines, C4, ammo box, claymores, radio beacon, T-UGS, repair tool...) and a specialization.
- **Squads and revives:** four to a squad, spawn on your squad mates, follow your leader's orders, and bring the fallen back with the defibrillator.
- **Ranks and stats like Battlefield 3:** 45 ranks and 100 Colonel service stars, kit scores that unlock each kit's guns and gadgets, attachments unlocked by kills, ribbons and medals, a round report every round, and a full service record of every gun, kit, vehicle and map.
- **Movement like BattleBit Remastered:** vault and mantle over walls and through windows, prone and dive to prone, slide, and lean round corners.
- **Bots that play like people:** they only know what they see and hear, react like a person, aim like a hand on a mouse, strafe, take cover and push, play in squads (revives, ammo, repairs, orders), and dig, build walls and bridges and tunnel under you.
- **Sound:** 3D sound that comes from where it is (round corners, muffled behind hills, late from far away), reverb for fields, streets, rooms and tunnels, punchy Battlefield guns and explosions, an original menu theme, and ambience for every map.
- **First person:** iron sights you aim down on every gun, red dots and holos you look through, and your own legs under you.
- **Split screen for four:** whichever you pick up, controller or keyboard and mouse, is player 1; more players join by holding Start on another controller. Every menu works on a controller.
- **Play together:** host a match for your home network, play online, or run a dedicated server for up to 64 players (32 against 32), with a window or without one (a VPS works).
- **A desktop app for Linux and Windows** that keeps itself up to date, or play the game file in any modern browser (WebGL 2).

## Maps

- **Classic maps** (512 × 512, Blue against Green): Classicgen, the original Ace of Spades generator, plus Hallway, Island, Mesa, Trenches and Arena.
- **Random maps:** a new Classicgen, Hills, Islands or City map from any seed, every match if you like.
- **Community maps:** twenty classic maps made by the Ace of Spades community (from [aos-server-mods](https://github.com/1AmYF/aos-server-mods) and [SpadesX](https://github.com/SpadesX/SpadesX)), downloaded the first time you play them and kept. Any `.vxl` map of your own works too.
- **Battlefield maps** (512 to 1,024 blocks across), in homage to the series' favourites (their layouts are our own, built from the originals' landmarks and flags):
  - *Battlefield 2: Modern Combat:* **Backstab**, **A Bridge Too Far**, **Harbor Edge** (a night raid on a river port), **Little Big Eye** (climb into the giant dish) and **Russian Border**.
  - *Bad Company:* **Oasis** and **Harvest Day**.
  - *Bad Company 2:* **Arica Harbor**, **Atacama Desert**, **Valparaiso**, **Port Valdez** and **Heavy Metal**.
  - *Battlefield 3:* **Caspian Border**, **Operation Firestorm**, **Wake Island**, **Strike at Karkand**, **Grand Bazaar**, **Operation Metro** (infantry only) and **Kharg Island**.
  - *Our own:* **Harbor** and **Frostbite**.

Every map plays Conquest, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Intel and Territorial Control.

## Download

Get the newest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/FartGarfunkel/Battle-of-Spades/releases/latest)**:

- **Linux:** `BattleOfSpades-…-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (`chmod +x`, or Properties → Allow executing as program) and run it.
- **Windows:** `BattleOfSpades-…-windows-x64.zip`. Unzip it anywhere and run `BattleOfSpades.exe`. If SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, choose More info → Run anyway.
- **Dedicated server:** `BattleOfSpades-…-server.zip`. Unzip it on any computer with Node.js 18 or newer and run `node bos-server.js --help`. Its `README.txt` explains how to keep it running on a VPS.
- **Just the game:** `battleofspades.html`. Open it in a browser, or install it in the desktop app with Options → Install update…

When a release only changes the game, the desktop apps stay on the release that brought them: its notes say which one.

## Updates

The desktop app checks for a new version each time the launcher opens and installs it by itself. When the app itself changes, Options offers **Update the app**: on Linux the AppImage is replaced where it is, and on Windows the new version is unpacked over the old one when the app restarts.

Your profiles, ranks, unlocks, loadouts and settings are never touched: they're kept in the app's own folder (`~/.config/BattleOfSpades` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\BattleOfSpades` on Windows).

## Dedicated server

- **With a window:** press **Dedicated Server** under Play in the launcher, or start the app with `--server`.
- **Without a window:** `./BattleOfSpades-…-x86_64.AppImage --headless-server --help` on Linux, or `BattleOfSpades-server.cmd --help` in the app's folder on Windows. On a machine with no desktop at all, use the server zip and Node.js.

Servers use TCP port 25590 (or the next free one up to 25599), and announce themselves on the local network on UDP port 25591, so players on the same network see them in the list without typing an address.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD | move |
| Space / Ctrl (hold) / Shift / V | jump / crouch / sprint / sneak |
| Space at a ledge, wall or window | vault or mantle over it or onto it (Battlefield rules) |
| C / Z | crouch (toggle) / prone (Battlefield rules; C crouches on classic rules) |
| Ctrl or C while sprinting / Z while sprinting | slide / dive to prone (Battlefield rules) |
| Q / E | lean left / right (Battlefield rules) |
| Left click | fire, hit with the spade, place a block, throw a grenade |
| Right click | aim; dig with the spade; build a line of blocks |
| 1 2 3 4 5 | spade, blocks, gun (press 3 again: your sidearm), grenades, your kit's gadgets (press 5 again: the second one) |
| ` | last tool |
| R | reload |
| Arrow keys, G, middle click | choose or pick a block colour (middle click spots on Battlefield rules unless you hold blocks) |
| F / C | get in or out of a vehicle / change seat (1 to 6 pick a seat) |
| V | first- or third-person view in a vehicle |
| B | change class or loadout |
| Tab / M | scoreboard / big map |
| X | spot an enemy |
| T / Y | chat / team chat |
| F11 or Alt+Enter | full screen (desktop app) |
| Esc | menu |

**Controllers** use Battlefield's console layout:

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick (click) / right stick | move (sprint) / look |
| RT / LT | fire / aim (dig, build a line) |
| A | jump, vault; in a vehicle: change seat |
| B | crouch (tap), prone (hold), slide (while sprinting); in a vehicle: camera |
| X | reload (hold: get in or out of a vehicle; with blocks out: pick a colour) |
| Y | switch between your primary and your sidearm |
| LB / RB | grenade / spot |
| R3 | spade |
| D-pad | spade, blocks, gadget, grenades (with blocks out: the colours) |
| Back / Start | scoreboard (tap: map) / menu |

Playing alone, whichever you pick up, controller or keyboard and mouse, is player 1. Hold Start on another controller to join as player 2, 3 or 4. In the menus, the D-pad or stick moves, A chooses, B goes back, LB/RB and LT/RT change tabs.

## Source code and building

Battle of Spades is plain JavaScript with no engine and no dependencies. The whole project travels inside the game file: `python3 release/restore.py release/battleofspades.html OUTDIR` rebuilds the project folder from it, `node tools/build.js` builds the game again, and `bash tools/package.sh` builds the desktop apps, the web app and the server zip.
